About Jason's Wood Refinishing

Locally owned and operated, proudly serving Fort Myers and the surrounding Southwest Florida community.

Our Story

A Craft Built on Patience and Pride

Jason's Wood Refinishing was built on a simple idea: good wood furniture deserves a second life. What started as a passion for restoring old, forgotten pieces has grown into a full-service refinishing business trusted by homeowners across Fort Myers, FL.

Every project — whether it's a single antique chair or a full set of kitchen cabinets — gets the same attention to detail, honest communication, and quality finish that the business was built on.
