Our Services

Full-service wood refinishing for furniture, cabinets, decks, and antiques — built for Southwest Florida's heat and humidity.

Service

Furniture Refinishing

Tables, chairs, dressers, buffets, and more — we strip old finishes, repair damage, sand to a smooth surface, and apply a new stain and topcoat that brings out the natural grain of the wood.

  • Hand-stripping of old paint, lacquer, or varnish
  • Structural repair of joints, veneer, and hardware
  • Custom stain matching and protective topcoats
Service

Cabinet Refinishing

Update your kitchen or bathroom without a full remodel. We refinish cabinet boxes, doors, and drawer fronts on-site or in-shop, with color-matching and durable finishes made for daily use.

  • Sanding, priming, and spray or hand-applied finishes
  • Stain or paint color changes
  • Hardware removal, reinstall, and minor repairs
Service

Deck Restoration

Florida sun, rain, and humidity are tough on outdoor wood. We pressure-wash, sand, repair, and seal decks, railings, and outdoor structures with finishes designed to hold up outdoors.

  • Pressure washing and wood brightening
  • Board repair and replacement
  • UV- and moisture-resistant staining/sealing
Service

Antique Restoration

Antiques call for a gentler touch. We assess each piece's age, construction, and finish to restore it in a way that preserves its character and value, not just its looks.

  • Careful assessment before any work begins
  • Period-appropriate finishing techniques
  • Repairs that protect long-term value
Service

Custom Staining & Finishing

Whether you're matching existing furniture or creating an entirely new look, we mix and test custom stain colors and finish sheens until they're exactly right.

  • Custom color matching and sample boards
  • Matte, satin, or gloss topcoat options
  • Durable, humidity-resistant finishes
